# ⚡ Atulya-Office (Powered by Atulya-Tantra)

> **A 100% Free, Private, Local-First AI Productivity Suite**  
> An open-source alternative to Microsoft Office, integrating Docs, Sheets, Slides, Proofing Studio, and local AI reasoning.

---

## 🌟 Overview

Atulya-Office is engineered to deliver zero-subscription, cloud-independent productivity with the privacy and speed of local inference.

* **📝 Tantra Docs**: Word alternative with distraction-free rich-text editing, automated summaries, and tone restructuring.
* **📊 Tantra Sheets**: Excel alternative with 500+ formulas, real-time grid computation, and natural language AI formula generation.
* **📽️ Tantra Slides**: PowerPoint alternative featuring instant prompt-to-deck building and presentation canvas.
* **🔍 Tantra Proof**: Proofing & language studio providing readability scoring, clarity improvements, and passive voice detection.
* **⚡ Tantra AI CoPilot**: Embedded slide-out assistant wired directly to the local `Tantra-LLM` engine.
